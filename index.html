Gum Paste Homogeneity

Thickener Paste Preparation

The V Mark Thickener Paste Preparation System provides perfect homogeneous mixing of gum paste, ensuring consistent quality of colour paste and eliminating batch-to-batch variation.

“Exact recipe can be followed easily. Each particle of all ingredients are mixed properly... very fast... very accurate, thanks to full timing control & variable speed control.”
  • Accurate Filling & Homogeneous Mixing: Prevents dry clumps and lumps from entering the printing screen.
  • Full Timing & Variable Speed Control: Allows tailored agitation programs for sodium alginate, synthetic thickeners, and guar gums.
  • Reduced Handling, Labour & Time: Heavy-duty transfer pump automates paste movement directly to storage tanks.
  • No-Spillage Approach: Keeps the department neat, clean, and tidy while saving on costly paste wastage.

Paste Straining Systems

Paste Preparation & Dispensing Systems

Vacuum Colour Strainer with Motorised Lifting & Tilting Device

Engineered for the most efficient & rapid straining of pigment, procion, reactive, discharge, and disperse printing pastes to obtain flawless, streak-free printing results. An easy and foolproof method of colour paste filtering that avoids choke-ups during rotary printing.

  • Filtering body in S.S. 304: Superior corrosion resistance against acidic and alkaline dye formulations.
  • Door seal in silicone rubber: Airtight vacuum seal resistant to aggressive textile chemicals and heat.
  • Facility for washing from upper part: Swift top-washout manifold cleans internal screens between color changes.
  • Liquid ring vacuum pump: Provides continuous, reliable vacuum pull through fine micron mesh.
  • Motorised lifting & tilting device: Effortless mechanical discharge of strained paste.
